Featured Milestones

From a small meeting in an Ohio car showroom to the biggest sporting event in America, the NFL has more than a century of history behind it. Start with these three moments that shaped the game.

1920

The APFA is founded in Canton, Ohio

Team owners met in Canton and formed the American Professional Football Association, which was renamed the National Football League two years later.

1967

The first Super Bowl is played

The Green Bay Packers beat the Kansas City Chiefs in the first AFL-NFL World Championship Game, later known as Super Bowl I.

1970

The AFL and NFL merge

The two rival leagues combined into one league with two conferences, creating the structure the NFL still uses today.
